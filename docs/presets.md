# Presets

Pick a preset from the selector at the top of the panel. It fills all 11 bands and refits the native filters instantly.

## Built-in presets

| Preset | Shapes |
| --- | --- |
| Flat | Everything at 0 dB |
| Bass Boost | Lifts the low end, tapers through the mids |
| Bass Reducer | Trims the low end |
| Treble Boost | Lifts the highs |
| Vocal Clarity | Scoops the lows, pushes presence |
| V-Shape | Boosts lows and highs, dips the mids |
| Warm | Gentle low-mid lift, soft highs |
| Bright | Leans into the upper mids and treble |
| Electronic | Punchy lows, crisp highs |
| Rock | Forward lows and treble, slight mid dip |
| Acoustic | Even lift across lows and presence |
| Loudness | Smile curve for quiet listening |
| Podcast | Centered on speech, rolls off the extremes |
| Lounge | Relaxed, slightly warm |

## Custom presets

1. Shape the curve however you like.
2. Click **Save preset**, type a name (up to 32 characters), and confirm.
3. Your preset appears under **My presets** in the selector.

Select a custom preset and click **Delete preset** to remove it. Custom presets are stored locally under the `moderneq:` storage namespace and survive restarts.

Editing any band after selecting a preset switches the selector to **Manual** so you always know whether what you hear is a named preset or your own edit.
